About Me

Hello! My name is Innocent Vomitadyo. I am a Ph.D. candidate in Agricultural and Resource Economics at Colorado State University.

My research focuses on environmental and natural resource economics, agricultural economics, groundwater management, and producer decision-making.

My current research examines the economic consequences of groundwater depletion and farmers' participation in groundwater conservation and land retirement programs.

I am particularly interested in applying econometric, economic modeling, spatial analysis, and input-output methods to environmental and agricultural policy questions.
